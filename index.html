Ko wai mātou · Who we are

Why we use barbering.

Tūranga Aiga is a Tauranga charitable trust with one clear mission: to equip rangatahi with skills that lead to real work. Barbering is our vehicle, because it draws in the young people others struggle to reach. They see it, and they want in.

Once a young person feels safe and knows they belong, we teach the trade, build lasting confidence, and weave in the hauora that holds the whole person together.

For those who want to keep going, we walk alongside them and refer them on to trusted training providers to earn a Level 4 barbering qualification.

✂

A real trade

A barbering skill they can take anywhere in the world, from first scissor control to precision fades and client ready work.

🤝

Belonging first

Belonging comes first for a reason. Rangatahi can only learn once they feel safe to be themselves. The chair creates that trust and respect. It is the space where we can truly engage the hard to reach and deliver everything else.

Hauora woven through

Built on Te Whare Tapa Whā: tinana, hinengaro, whānau and wairua. Self care and wellbeing run through every day, holding the whole young person together as they learn.

Te hōtaka · The programme

Barbering, from first cut to career.

A 12 module journey built around the trade. Each day runs 9:30am to 3:00pm with time on the tools, cutting, fades and client work, alongside hauora, self care and reflection that keep rangatahi strong. It closes with a public showcase, a CV, and a plan for their next step.

Te taha hauora · Caring for the whole person

Hauora and self care, woven through every day.

A haircut is the doorway. Hauora is what holds a young person together once they walk through it. Every day we make space to check in, to breathe, and to care for the whole person. We follow Te Whare Tapa Whā, the four walls of a strong wharenui. When all four are cared for, a rangatahi stands tall.

💪

Taha tinana · Body

Good kai, movement and rest. We share a meal together every day, and we talk about looking after the body that carries you.

🧠

Taha hinengaro · Mind

Space to think and feel. We practise simple ways to calm the mind, name what is going on inside, and build steady self belief.

🫂

Taha whānau · Family

Nobody stands alone. We build a crew that has your back, and we welcome whānau in so the support carries on at home.

✨

Taha wairua · Spirit

Knowing who you are and where you come from. Karakia, identity and purpose give rangatahi something solid to stand on.

Simple self care we practise together

  • Daily check ins, so nobody carries a hard day on their own.
  • Karakia and reflection to open and close each day with calm.
  • Shared kai, because relationships grow over food.
  • Breathing and grounding tools for tough moments.
  • Journaling to track growth and celebrate small wins.
  • Kanohi ki te kanohi kōrero, real talk, face to face.

Te ara whakamua · The road ahead

From our chair into further study.

This is the part that makes us proud. Rangatahi who start with a first haircut go on to real qualifications and real careers. Once they finish with us, we do not just wave them off. We walk beside them and refer them on to trusted training providers so they can keep growing. Here are two great next steps.

Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology

A trusted training provider we refer rangatahi on to. Toi Ohomai runs hairdressing and barbering study right here in Tauranga at the Windermere campus, including a hands on Level 4 barbering pathway taught in a real, on campus barber shop. It is a natural next step for a young person ready to earn a nationally recognised qualification.

  • Hairdressing and barbering study
  • Tauranga, Windermere campus
  • Student finance support available
Explore Toi Ohomai courses

Havana Barbers

A working barber shop and training space in Mount Maunganui where rangatahi can build real hours on the tools with clients. It is a great option for a young person who learns best hands on, in the chair, in a genuine barber shop environment. Get in touch to ask about training and opportunities.

  • Hands on barber shop training
  • 5B Totara Street, Mount Maunganui
  • Open Tuesday to Friday

    Taku kōrero · My story

    Why I do this work.

    I know this road, because I walked it. I came from a dark place. Little hope, plenty of potential, no support. Like a lot of our rangatahi, I grew up surrounded by the wrong influences, and for a long time that was all I could see.

    Then I hit the end of the road. A one way street with no way out. I knew I had to change, and I knew I had to break the cycles and the generational curses that had run through my family for years. So I made a decision. No more excuses. And I found my way out through barbering.

    But I did not do it alone. What turned my life around was my faith, a righteous belief that gave me a foundation to stand on and a reason to keep going when everything else told me to give up. I also found people who believed in me and stood in my corner when I could not stand on my own. Together that gave me the strength to climb out and become the man I am today.

    Having someone in your corner changes everything. That is exactly who I am for our rangatahi now. I am the one in their corner, believing in them until they learn to believe in themselves.

    So I use barbering to reach rangatahi who remind me of myself. I know it worked for me, and I know it works for them. There is something sacred in cutting someone's hair. In te ao Māori the hair is tapu, it carries mana and energy, and in that chair something real opens up. Walls come down. Kōrero flows. A young person starts to feel like they belong to something bigger than the streets they came from.

    That is what I give them. A craft they can carry anywhere in the world, a safe space to speak and be heard, and the trust that breaks the chains. I want to raise up great men and great women, leaders of our community, pioneers of their own future, and walk them from our barber chair out into the world. That is why I do this work.

    Mō ngā whānau · For families

    Questions whānau often ask.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing for referred rangatahi. The programme is 100% funded, including the barber kit your rangatahi keeps.

    Who can join?

    Rangatahi aged 15 to 24 in the Tauranga area. No experience or NCEA credits needed, just a willingness to show up.

    Where and when does it run?

    In Tauranga Moana, weekdays from 9:30am to 3:00pm across a 12 module course. We'll confirm the venue and next start date when you get in touch.

    Is it just barbering?

    Barbering is the heart of it. Alongside it we weave in hauora, identity and belonging, grounded in Māori and Pasifika values, so your rangatahi grows as a whole person.

    Does it lead anywhere?

    Yes. Rangatahi leave with a CV, a strong foundation of barbering skills, and a plan for what comes next. For those keen to go further, we refer them on to trusted training providers like Toi Ohomai to earn a Level 4 qualification, and we back them into work, apprenticeships or running their own barber shop.

    How do I support my rangatahi to enrol?

    Email us on their behalf, or encourage them to reach out themselves. We'll meet you kanohi ki te kanohi and answer any pātai you have.

    Who is the tutor?

    Tūranga Aiga is led by Shanon Ratahi, a master barber, educator and community mentor with more than 15 years of experience and his own barbering space. Shanon previously tutored barbering at Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology, then stepped out to build kaupapa designed specifically for rangatahi. He teaches from lived experience, having broken his own generational cycles through barbering, and now uses the chair to reach young people others struggle to reach. Grounded in tuakana teina and a deep commitment to whānau, Shanon walks alongside every rangatahi. Read his story.
